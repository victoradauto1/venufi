# VenueFi

VenueFi is a Real World Asset (RWA) revenue-sharing protocol that enables operators to raise capital from investors, deploy it into real-world businesses, and distribute revenue proportionally back to investors on-chain.

The protocol is composed of Solidity smart contracts and a Next.js frontend that allows investors and operators to interact directly with deployed contracts on Ethereum Sepolia.

---

## Overview

VenueFi follows a simple revenue-sharing model:

1. An operator creates a venue campaign.
2. Investors contribute ETH during the funding phase.
3. If the funding goal is reached, the campaign becomes active.
4. The operator withdraws the capital and deploys it into a real-world business.
5. Revenue generated by the business is deposited on-chain.
6. Investors claim revenue proportionally to their ownership share.
7. The campaign eventually reaches its end state.

Unlike tokenized RWA protocols, VenueFi uses non-transferable internal shares rather than ERC20 tokens, simplifying accounting and reducing protocol complexity.

* Investor balances
* Revenue distribution state

---

## Core Features

### Funding Campaigns

Operators can create independent venue campaigns with:

* Funding goal
* Funding duration
* Operating duration
* Operator fee percentage

### Revenue Sharing

Revenue is distributed proportionally to all investors using an accumulator-based accounting model.

### Refund Protection

If a campaign fails to reach its funding goal before the deadline:

* Funding expires
* Investors can reclaim their full investment

### Permissionless State Progression

Critical state transitions can be executed by anyone when conditions are met, preventing campaigns from becoming stuck.
